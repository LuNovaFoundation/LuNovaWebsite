About LuNova

Our Story

Born from a belief that every child deserves to shine.

LuNova was founded in 2026 by a small group of educators, designers, and advocates who saw a critical gap in resources available to neurodivergent children in Vietnam. With limited access to specialized tools and support systems, many children were falling through the cracks of a system not yet built for them.

We started with a simple question: What if we designed something just for them? That question became our Gearboard project — and the beginning of LuNova.

Sensory Tools · Vietnam

Gearboards for Neurodivergent Children

Our first and flagship initiative, the Gearboard Project designs and distributes custom sensory activity boards to neurodivergent children in Vietnam who lack access to occupational therapy resources. Each gearboard is handcrafted with age-appropriate sensory components to support focus, fine motor development, and self-regulation.
